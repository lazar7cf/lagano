# Cafe Lagano

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Introduce Cafe Lagano and help visitors browse the drinks and prices, find opening hours, and get directions to Marktplatz, 71063 Sindelfingen.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing stack is static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Preserve `index.html`, `menu.html`, menu category anchors, search, responsive navigation, opening status, map and directions. `assets/data/menu.json` is the source of truth for all seven categories and 83 entries. Never invent products, prices, ingredients, business claims, reviews, contact details or destinations.

## Brand Commitments

The user requests an evolution of Cafe Lagano: a distinctive contemporary European neighbourhood café with relaxed Balkan character, warm natural color, editorial typography and café photography. Keep German copy conversational. Avoid luxury restaurant, SaaS, beige café template, repeated component layouts, excessive labels, cards, pills, gradients and decorative icons. The name and existing lowercase serif wordmark are recognizable assets.

## Evidence on Hand

`CAFE-LAGANO-CODEX-REDESIGN.md` is the user's brand brief. Business address and regular opening hours are retained from the existing site. Locally stored café photographs are temporary stock images, documented in `REDESIGN-NOTES.md`; they must not be presented as actual venue, staff or customers. Real photography is not available. Phone, email, reservation and Instagram destinations remain unconfirmed and must not be invented.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, semantic headings, descriptive image alternatives, readable contrast, reduced motion, adequate touch targets and complete static menu access without JavaScript.
