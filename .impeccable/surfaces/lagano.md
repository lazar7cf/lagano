# Cafe Lagano — homepage and drinks

Modes: homepage Persuade; drinks Read / Operate.

## Direction contract

**Thesis.** Café window lettering and a table shared with friends. Evolve Lagano's recognizable serif wordmark and warm photographs; remove the repeated label / serif / italic / split-section formula.

**Own-world.** Deep olive fields, warm paper, charcoal, a restrained clay accent. Archivo gives the sturdy voice of café signage; Instrument Serif remains the wordmark and occasional conversational headline. Rectangular photographs, text links, useful menu rules, no decorative containers.

**FORM.** Café window lettering: sturdy flush-left signage, the preserved serif wordmark, a headline spanning the photograph and description, and an open typographic menu rail. Concept seed `3d5196cd` assigned grounded candidate `6` for this direction (API source, Persuade mode, pool `c3b204a1eed6`). The existing café brief pins the palette and cultural character.

**Story.** A shared coffee, the meaning of Lagano and Balkan hospitality, real drinks and prices, then opening hours and directions. Merge redundant introduction, reasons and visit sections.

**First viewport.** A typographic line across an olive field, then a broad coffee photograph with a narrow description and clear drinks action beside it. On phones the heading, photograph and action are recomposed into one compact sequence. The drinks page opens with a short title, a small photograph, category navigation and quickly accessible rows.

**Signature interaction.** The category underline follows the reading position, brings hidden categories into the horizontal mobile rail, and a category choice resets search to reveal the full section. Navigation is a short accessible dropdown, not a fullscreen takeover.

**Motion and reach.** A single, already visible photograph settles into place, with reduced-motion fallback. Shared lettering, color, controls and spacing join both routes. Price data stays generated from the existing JSON. Photography can be replaced without structural changes.

## Selection

The user delegated the design decisions and requested direct implementation. Build directly in code, using the existing photographs; no additional approval round or generated concept images. This is a session choice, not a stored workflow preference.

The concept seed assigned grounded candidate 6: café window lettering. Other grounded sources considered: table menu, neighbourhood noticeboard, café journal, terrace furniture, coffee packaging and a drinks ledger. The pinned café brief controls the palette and cultural character. Swiss poster discipline is competitive for clarity and contributes a stronger grid and type hierarchy. Racing livery, one-bit desktop, oscilloscope, indoor weather sun and jacquard brocade lose on audience identification and café task clarity; keep their discipline of consistent identity, legible states, information precision, decisive color and cross-surface consistency, without their motifs.
